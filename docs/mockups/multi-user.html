Revocation
Revoking a participant stops their requests and live event delivery, including on an already connected client. It does not revoke the run's provider credential or affect other participants.
  • 1Tiered access. Anyone granted access starts as an observer (read-only) until the owner approves them to participant.
  • 2Approval is the only gate. After approval a participant chats, directs the agent, and approves tool actions without asking again.
  • 3New people join as observers. Granting access defaults to the read-only tier; promotion is a separate owner action.
  • 4Invitations stay pending. Before a person connects there is no participant at all.
  • 5Revocation is scoped. It cuts the participant's live delivery without touching the credential or the run.
